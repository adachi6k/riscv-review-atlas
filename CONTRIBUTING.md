# Contributing

Use English for documentation and issue reports. Work from public, attributable evidence.

Before adding an entry, check the repository investigation issue and existing questions for duplicates. Include source links and revisions. Distinguish upstream reports from independently reproduced results, and document applicability and uncertainty.

Do not submit credentials, private repository content, personal data unrelated to technical evidence, or wholesale copies of third-party discussions/source. Linked upstream content remains under its original terms. Submissions of original material are under this repository's MIT license.

Research automation may assist collection and evaluation. Describe its role and verify factual claims. Publishing an investigation here does not authorize opening issues or contacting maintainers in upstream projects.
